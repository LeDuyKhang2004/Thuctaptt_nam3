COMMUNICATION JOB

By the end, a university project-review audience should understand that LeaveFlow is a complete role-based web application that centralizes leave requests, approval decisions, and contextual AI assistance because the system combines a clear workflow, secure data access, and a tested Java/Spring architecture.

NARRATIVE ARC

1. Introduce the project and author.
2. Establish the presentation structure.
3. Explain why manual leave coordination creates avoidable delays and uncertainty.
4. Define the project's measurable functional and quality objectives.
5. Show the implementation stack and layered architecture.
6. Explain the Employee/Admin roles and the request lifecycle.
7. Show how the database preserves ownership and review history.
8. Present the most valuable user, admin, and AI capabilities.
9. Transition to a live demonstration.
10. Summarize implemented results and realistic next improvements.
11. Synthesize the technical and user value of the project.
12. Invite questions.

OUTPUT LENGTH

12 slides, appropriate for a concise 8–12 minute project report plus demonstration.
